# Adaptive progression

Adaptive state is per child and skill/domain. `heuristic-v1` uses repeated rolling evidence rather than one answer: strong fluent evidence opens an immediate-successor probe; repeated probe success advances a frontier; weak evidence stays targeted and examines prerequisites/components. A failed probe never demotes unrelated skills.

Initial bands are configurable hypotheses: strong ≥95% timely evidence probes, ≥90% progresses gradually, 80–90% is productive, 65–80% targeted practice, and below 65% reduces complexity/falls back. These are not calibrated claims. Normal automatic probes are limited to immediate successors of current/prior curriculum; parents must opt in to farther-ahead manual work.
