The cited syllabus says primary mathematics establishes basic numeracy, reasoning/problem solving, confidence and interest. Its framework centres mathematical problem solving. Math Drill is deliberately a narrow complement: sustained retrieval and execution after teaching, not a representation of the whole framework.

## Official organisation—not Math Drill skill names

The official content is organised at least as **school level → strand → sub-strand → content/learning objective**. The three strands are:

1. Number and Algebra
2. Measurement and Geometry
3. Statistics

The current programme is cumulative. P1–P4 is common; P5–P6 have Standard and Foundation pathways, with Foundation revisiting key earlier concepts and introducing a subset of Standard content. Store pathway as curriculum context; never infer ability/difficulty from it.

At a high level, levels move from P1 whole numbers/operations/money, shapes/measurement/time, and simple data; through P2–P4 larger whole numbers, four operations, fractions/decimals, money, measurement/geometry/statistics; to P5–P6 fractions, decimals, percentage, ratio/rate, algebraic notation, area/volume/geometry and data analysis. ### Initial mapping boundary

Implement enough P1–P4-related retrieval skills to be useful (number bonds, four-operation facts/mental computation, multi-digit procedure components, selected fraction/decimal/conversion fluency). Do not announce MOE completion. P5/P6 data can be ingested for progression/prerequisite context before all its generators exist.

## Assessment interpretation

MOE describes assessment as an ongoing teaching/learning process. Its syllabus distinguishes:

- **Summative assessment**: measures what students have learned, commonly score/grade reported.
- **Formative and diagnostic assessment**: assessment for learning, used to provide timely feedback on learning and teaching; classroom assessment is primarily formative/diagnostic.

Math Drill’s `PRACTICE`, `REVIEW`, and `PROBE` are formative evidence; a `TEST` is a bounded product activity, not an official school examination. Parent reports must say “Math Drill evidence” and must not make school-placement, diagnosis, or curriculum-completion claims.

## Classification policy

Each imported objective has an explicit product classification:

- `FLUENCY_CORE`: repeated retrieval/execution and efficiency are central (e.g. fact families, number bonds, mental arithmetic).
- `FLUENCY_SUPPORTING`: repeatable execution is useful but speed is less central (e.g. conversions, fractions/decimals, multi-digit procedures, angle recognition).
- `REFERENCE_ONLY`: retained as context but not claimed assessed/taught (open problem solving, investigations, communication, construction/reasoning that needs a lesson context).

Classification is Math Drill’s pedagogical judgment, never presented as MOE terminology.
