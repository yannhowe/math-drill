# Skill graph

Internal skills are a directed graph, not a school-year tree. Initial arithmetic facts include operation facts and fact-family/missing-operand relationships. Relations are `PREREQUISITE`, `COMPONENT`, `SUCCESSOR`, and `RELATED`; only prerequisite/component evidence may inform targeted fallback. Relations are source-controlled data and prerequisite cycles are rejected in tests.

The P4-focused starter graph intentionally remains small: facts → missing-number fact families → mental/multi-digit computation. It is an internal decomposition and must never be labelled official MOE terminology.
